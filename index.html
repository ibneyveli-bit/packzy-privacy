Privacy Policy for Packzy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Packzy (also referred to as "Family Trip Planner" / "Family Tour & Logistics Planner") is a mobile application developed by Balasundaram Iyyadurai ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Packzy collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. This policy applies to the Packzy app available on Google Play and the App Store.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • If you sign in with Apple (iOS): we receive your name and email address (or a private relay email if you choose to hide your email) as provided by Apple, and use Firebase Authentication to create your account.
  • If you sign in with Google (Android): we receive your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by Google, and use Firebase Authentication to create your account.
  • If you continue as a guest (iOS and Android): no personal information is collected. An anonymous account identifier is created locally so your data can be stored, with no name or email attached.

Trip and Packing List Data

Destinations, packing list items, checklist status, and trip dates that you enter in the app. This data is stored on your device and, if you are signed in, synced to your private Firebase Cloud Firestore account so it is available across your devices.

Location Data

If you use the "My Location" feature, the app accesses your device's precise location (via device GPS) only while you are actively using that feature. This location is used to show your coordinates and to let you optionally share your location (e.g. via WhatsApp or Google Maps) at your own initiative. Location data is not collected in the background and is not stored on our servers.

Documents (Document Vault)

Photos or documents you add to the in-app Document Vault (e.g. passports, tickets) are stored locally on your device only, encrypted at rest. These files are never uploaded to our servers or any third party.

Device and App Information

Basic app version information (via package info) is read locally to check for app updates. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in
  • To store and sync your trip and packing list data across your devices
  • To provide features you actively use, such as location sharing, currency conversion, and PDF export of your packing lists
  • To respond to support requests

Data Storage and Security

Account and trip data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google), and locally on your device using encrypted, app-scoped storage. Document Vault files use on-device encryption and are never transmitted off your device. Each user's data is isolated and accessible only to that user's authenticated account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is processed by Firebase/Google Cloud solely as our infrastructure provider to operate the app's authentication and sync features.

Information is shared with third parties (such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, or your device's share sheet) only when you explicitly choose to share it through an in-app share action.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Guest data stored only on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Children's Privacy

Packzy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
